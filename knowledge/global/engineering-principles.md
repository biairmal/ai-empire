# Engineering Principles

These apply to every project, stack, human, and agent.

1. **Human-controlled authority.** Humans make the final decisions at defined gates.
2. **Autonomous execution.** Authorized, low-risk work proceeds without asking.
3. **Explicit approval gates.** Decision boundaries are declared, not improvised.
4. **Project isolation.** Knowledge and files of one project never leak into another.
5. **Technology-stack isolation.** Stack knowledge applies only to that stack.
6. **Versioned engineering knowledge.** Knowledge lives in Git with history.
7. **Minimal context loading.** Load only what the task needs.
8. **Explicit relationships between knowledge artifacts.** Link documents with typed relationships, not loose mentions.
9. **Auditable AI actions.** Every meaningful action leaves a record.
10. **Replaceable AI models.** No permanent dependency on one provider.
11. **Least privilege.** Grant the minimum access required.
12. **Simple over clever.**
13. **Explicit over magical.**
14. **Versioned over implicit.**
15. **Isolated over shared.**
16. **Reusable knowledge over repeated prompting.**
17. **Avoid unnecessary infrastructure complexity.**
